# Hidden Spots Register

These are opportunities to investigate, not automatic feature commitments.

## High-signal product gaps

### 1. Handoff recovery
Today the strongest product law is FINAL-only custody. The next real-world gap is what happens when the next bridge cannot continue.

Questions:
- Can a current holder safely decline after accepting?
- Can the creator reroute without confusing custody?
- How does an expired/abandoned route recover?
- What evidence survives rerouting?

### 2. Trusted people memory
A repeated handoff should not require rebuilding social trust from zero.

Possible mechanism:
- previous verified collaborators;
- private trusted circle;
- “used before” context;
- no public social graph by default.

### 3. Custody passport
The route can become a memorable artifact rather than a log.

Potential primitives:
- stamped handoff moments;
- current custodian marker;
- “who accepted / when / proof class”;
- arrival receipt;
- replayable route story.

### 4. Repeat and recurring routes
Potential jobs:
- event equipment;
- keys/fobs;
- neighborhood item transfer;
- team equipment;
- recurring volunteer/community logistics.

### 5. Responsibility context capsule
A handoff often needs context beyond “send this onward.”

Explore:
- purpose;
- constraints;
- handling notes;
- deadline;
- contact boundary;
- what must remain private.

Do not allow this to become an unbounded sensitive-data store.

### 6. Bridge availability / fallback
A useful bridge is not just trusted; they must be able to act.

Explore:
- available / not available;
- expiry;
- “cannot continue”;
- safe fallback;
- creator notification.

### 7. Activation narrative
Current registration is easy, but users still need a reason to open NimCarry in Nimiq Pay.

Potential framing:
- “Verify your handoff identity”
- “Make your first handoff provable”
rather than “connect a crypto wallet.”

### 8. Proof that is useful outside NimCarry
Could a privacy-safe route receipt be shared with a marketplace seller, event organizer, property manager, or team without exposing private route data?

### 9. Team mode
Potential higher-value buyer:
- property managers;
- events/festivals;
- clubs/communities;
- local businesses;
- volunteer networks;
- equipment-heavy teams.

### 10. Failed-handoff intelligence
If routes fail repeatedly at a specific step, that is a product signal.

Instrument:
- invite ignored;
- provider unavailable;
- wallet verification abandoned;
- pass attempt not broadcast;
- bridge declined;
- route expired;
- reroute succeeded/failed.

## Deliberately deferred

Do not rush into:
- medication delivery;
- regulated goods;
- cross-border commercial parcel forwarding;
- anonymous stranger matching;
- speculative token incentives;
- public reputation scores;
- insurance-like guarantees.

Each creates legal, safety, fraud, or trust complexity before the base behavior is validated.
