# Security, Abuse & Trust Questions

NimCarry's strongest product value depends on trust. Growth mechanisms must not outrun the trust model.

## Abuse surfaces to monitor

- scripted registrations;
- wallet farming;
- fake activation using controlled wallets;
- collusive route loops;
- stolen/borrowed wallet access;
- spam invitations;
- harassment through repeated invites;
- sensitive notes entered into mission context;
- bridge coercion;
- disputed physical custody despite digital proof;
- malicious rerouting;
- fraudulent team accounts.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Registration = acquisition.
2. Explicit consent = privacy/accountability.
3. Valid Nimiq signature = wallet control.
4. Post-verification meaningful mission action = activation.
5. Independently FINAL hop = strongest protocol evidence.
6. Repeat FINAL routes by the same real network = stronger retention evidence.

## Product rules to preserve

- approval ≠ broadcast ≠ FINAL;
- browser claims cannot move custody;
- no public email exposure;
- no public route graph by default;
- old activity cannot be relabeled as new traction;
- rate limiting is abuse mitigation, not proof of humanity.

## Before bridge discovery

Require a separate safety review covering:
- identity/trust model;
- reporting/blocking;
- prohibited goods;
- location privacy;
- stranger contact;
- fraud economics;
- liability language;
- jurisdictional constraints.

## Before compensation

Review:
- whether payment changes the activity into regulated commercial delivery;
- dispute handling;
- refund/failure rules;
- tax/reporting implications;
- incentive gaming.
