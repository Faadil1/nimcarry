## Final ≤250-word description

Warm introductions disappear after the first handoff. NimCarry turns them into a consented, verifiable human route.

NimCarry is a Nimiq Pay Mini App for people who need to reach someone they cannot contact directly. A creator defines a known destination and invites a trusted human bridge. The bridge explicitly accepts before any payment is attempted. Exactly 1 NIM becomes the custody baton—not a reward, wager, stake, or game mechanic. Each verified handoff moves the baton one human closer to the destination.

The experience is simple: create a mission, invite a bridge, accept the invitation, approve the 1 NIM pass, and follow the route. NimCarry waits for independent chain verification; wallet approval, a pending transaction, or a browser claim never changes custody. Only FINAL advances the route. When the destination becomes the finalized recipient, the mission becomes ARRIVED and produces a privacy-safe Route Receipt.

Nimiq Pay is essential rather than decorative: it provides the wallet session, account context, explicit user approval, and transaction submission path that make each human handoff a real custody event. The app keeps the destination protected while the route remains understandable.

NimCarry is released as a Cycle II Public Preview. The deterministic demo shows the product story, while real TESTNET A→B→C proof remains pending because two controlled Nimiq Pay 2.19.1 TESTNET attempts reached approval but produced no independently observed broadcast evidence. No real FINAL or ARRIVED is claimed.
