## Final ≤250-word description

Warm introductions disappear after the first handoff. Once someone says “I’ll pass it on,” the creator usually loses visibility into who carries the introduction next, whether that person consented, and whether the handoff actually happened.

NimCarry turns that invisible chain into a consented, destination-bound human route. A creator defines a known destination and invites a trusted bridge. The bridge explicitly accepts before any payment is attempted. Exactly 1 NIM becomes the custody baton—not a reward, wager, stake, or game mechanic. Each verified handoff moves the baton one human closer to the destination.

Nimiq Pay is central to the experience: it provides wallet context, explicit user approval, and the transaction path for the handoff. NimCarry then verifies chain evidence independently. Wallet approval, a pending transaction, or a browser claim never changes custody; only FINAL advances the route. When the destination becomes the finalized recipient, the mission becomes ARRIVED and produces a privacy-safe Route Receipt.

The production app, backend, persistence layer, security boundaries, and Guided Demo are live. The deterministic demo shows the full product story while real TESTNET A→B→C proof remains pending: two controlled Nimiq Pay 2.19.1 attempts reached approval but produced no independently observed broadcast evidence. No real FINAL or ARRIVED is claimed.
