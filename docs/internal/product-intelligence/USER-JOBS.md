# User Jobs

## Core functional job

> When something or a responsibility must reach a person I cannot directly meet, help me move it through willing people while preserving clear responsibility and proof of each handoff.

## Emotional jobs

- I want to know the handoff did not disappear into a chat thread.
- I want confidence that the next person actually agreed.
- I do not want to chase everyone manually.
- I want proof without exposing the whole route publicly.
- I want recovery when someone can no longer continue.

## Social jobs

- Demonstrate that I handled my part responsibly.
- Avoid arguments about who had custody.
- Coordinate with people I already trust.
- Give a recipient or organizer a simple proof that the route completed.

## Candidate wedges to validate

| Wedge | Why NimCarry may fit | What must be learned |
|---|---|---|
| Keys / fobs / access items | custody matters and handoffs are common | legal/safety expectations; frequency |
| Marketplace pickup handoff | seller/buyer may use a trusted intermediary | whether users want proof vs simple chat |
| Event equipment | multi-person temporary custody is common | team workflow and repeat frequency |
| Community/volunteer logistics | trusted networks already exist | coordination burden and willingness to adopt |
| Forgotten-item return | destination is known but direct meetup is hard | urgency, trust, value threshold |
| Team equipment / assets | repeated accountability | willingness to pay and admin needs |
| Documents that are not legally sensitive | chain-of-custody can matter | privacy/legal limits |

## Interview prompts

- Tell me about the last time you needed something to reach someone through another person.
- What was the most stressful part?
- How did you know who had it?
- What happened if someone stopped responding?
- What proof would have been useful?
- Would you use the same intermediaries again?
- What would make you refuse to use a product like NimCarry?
- Who would pay when this happens repeatedly?
