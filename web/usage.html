Evidence boundary

Registration is not a wallet claim. A person can register with name + email and explicit privacy consent without Nimiq. A wallet is counted only after a real Nimiq signature. Protocol participation is counted only when a registered user's verified wallet appears in protocol participation records.

Pre-registry development/test participation rows are intentionally excluded from the registered-user participation metric. Screenshots containing user emails are not published as evidence.
